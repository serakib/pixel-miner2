⛏ How to Play

🎯 Goal

Explore the cave, collect resources, survive crawlers and reach the green exit to descend to the next depth.

🕹 Controls

WASD / Arrow Keys move. You can also click an adjacent tile. On mobile, use the D-pad.

⛏ Mining

Walking into a rock or resource tile mines it. Rocks cost a little energy; valuable minerals give more score.

💎 Resources

Copper = 15 points. Crystal = 40 points. Energy cells restore energy.

❤️ Survival

Cave crawlers chase you. Avoid them. Hazards reduce HP. If HP or energy reaches zero, the expedition ends.

⬇️ Depth

Every green exit takes you deeper. Deeper levels become more dangerous and spawn more crawlers.
